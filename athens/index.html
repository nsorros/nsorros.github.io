// athens

A friend’s guide to Athens.

The places I send friends to when they visit. Not a top-ten list — just where I actually drink coffee, eat, and walk. Every name opens in Google Maps; the whole thing is also one saved list.

[01] // neighbourhoods
  • Kypseli Diverse, cultural and up-and-coming. →
  • Pangrati A more developed, clean and posh version of Kypseli. →
  • Plaka The old Athens. →
  • Anafiotika This spot next to the Parthenon is like going to a Greek island. A very unique experience. →
[02] // coffee
  • KICK The coffee spot we work from. →
  • Anana Coffee & Food An equally nice coffee shop in a more central location. →
  • Dope Roasting Co Some London vibes, also in the centre. →
  • Taf Coffee The first specialty coffee shop in Athens, so it influenced many of the others. →
  • LOT 51 A bit further from the centre, but quite hippy and nice. →
  • The Underdog Legendary coffee shop that has won many awards worldwide. →
  • Samba Coffee Roasters I personally really like the coffee here. It’s a small chain, so there are a few Sambas around. →
[03] // concept stores
  • Minu A really nice place in a traditional old Athenian house. All the furniture is design furniture they also sell, plenty of plants, and food and coffee. →
  • Paraphernalia Most of the furniture in our house is from here. →
  • Plegma Coffee & Living Cafe. →
[04] // food
  • Lisa Pizza. The pizza place in Kypseli. →
  • Pizza Dough! An equally nice pizza, more central. Also has natural wines. →
  • Django Gelato Unbelievable ice cream. →
  • Fita Fine dining, but toned down and for our age. Very recommended. →
  • Dylan (ΝΤΥΛΑΝ) Same idea as Fita, but in Kypseli. →
  • Annie – Fine Cooking The menu changes every day and the food is amazing. Also has natural wines. →
  • Linou Soumpasis & Sia Alternative fine dining. The best of all the options, super recommended. →
[05] // bakeries
  • KORA Only sourdough bread, quite famous for it. A bit pricey. →
  • Flake The place opposite KICK. →
  • Neropistolo New entrant in town, on my list to go. →
  • Asterisque Pastry shop. →
[06] // drinks
  • Eprepe Bar Wine bar. →
  • Wine is fine Wine bar. →
  • Materia Prima Bistro, in Pangrati. →
  • Paleo Wine Store Wine bar. →
[07] // art
  • EMST National Museum of Contemporary Art Athens. →
  • Acropolis Museum Really worth going. All the Parthenon marbles are displayed there. →
  • Benaki Museum Ancient Greek history. →
  • Goulandris Foundation Amazing art collection. Very famous paintings that one mega-rich Greek collected. →
[08] // experiences
  • Open-air cinema Summer.
  • Parthenon
  • Walk Plaka & Anafiotika Links in neighbourhoods.
  • Kypseli or Pangrati Explore either for a true Athenian neighbourhood.
[09] // kids
  • Stavros Niarchos Park Open and beautiful space for both grown-ups and little ones. →
  • XPLORE Entertainment Center A super big indoor entertainment space. A bit too much to go often, but super fun for one time. →
  • The Ellinikon Experience Park The old airport, now redeveloped as a park, play and residential area. →
